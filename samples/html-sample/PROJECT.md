# Signal Garden

A self-contained HTML sample for Frameground. Signal Garden is a quiet plant-care journal for people who prefer a small, attentive ritual over a productivity dashboard.

## Frames

- Today: the next small care action and a calm overview
- Plant detail: one plant's light, moisture and history
- Care plan: a future-facing care rhythm
- Journal: observations and a clear empty state
- Collection: a tactile inventory of plants

No external services, fonts, images or dependencies are required. Start from `frames.json` in Frameground.
